Model notes

Road capacity is lane count × 6 vehicle agents unless a scenario sets an explicit capacity. Travel time is road length ÷ speed limit (rounded up) in discrete ticks. Red signals and a full downstream road create queues. No lane changing, collision avoidance or calibration. Grade separation is represented by separate graph nodes and ramp connectivity, not by a 3D model.
